# Design requirements

## North star

As perfect as Linear's website or Apple Notes:

- Simple purpose
- Simple architecture
- Simple design
- Simple implementation

## Rules

- Cut taglines, helper text and secondary labels.
- If a screen needs them to make sense, fix the layout instead.
- Every decision must elevate user experience (UX), developer experience (DX)
  and agent experience (AX) at once — without breaking anything.
