# Human handoff

Owner: Customer support team.

Create a support ticket when [[Answer checks]] cannot approve the draft. A support specialist reviews the evidence and writes the final reply.

{{diagram:Handoff}}

## Ticket contents

- The customer's original question.
- Retrieved excerpts and their source IDs.
- The proposed answer, if one exists.
- The reason automatic delivery stopped.
- The product and documentation versions.

For the restoration question, record: "The available documentation covers workspace exports. No approved restoration policy was found."

## Completion

The specialist verifies the applicable policy, responds to the customer, and adds missing documentation when appropriate. Both the automatic and reviewed paths finish at Customer reply.
