# Answer checks

Owner: Support platform team.

An answer reaches the customer automatically only when its factual claims have supporting excerpts and its proposed action follows the documented support policy.

{{diagram:Quality}}

## Decision table

| Evidence | Policy | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Citations support the answer | Action is documented | Follow yes to Customer reply. |
| A claim has no supporting excerpt | Any | Follow no to [[Human handoff]]. |
| Citations support the answer | Action needs a specialist | Follow no to [[Human handoff]]. |

Check citations first, then policy. Ready to send? combines those results. The demo animates both outgoing branches to explain the available paths; playback does not evaluate the customer's question.

## Example outcomes

"How do I export my workspace?" can follow yes using exports-01 and exports-02.

"Can you restore a workspace that I deleted last year?" follows no because the demo provides no restoration policy. See [[Retrieval runbook]].
